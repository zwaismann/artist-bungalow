# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Prospective guests evaluating The Artist Bungalow from Instagram, Linktree,
search, or Airbnb-adjacent links. Most are browsing on phones and deciding
whether the property feels distinctive, trustworthy, and easy to book. A
secondary audience is confirmed guests using the digital guidebook on mobile
before arrival and during the stay.

## Product Purpose

The site is a cinematic single-page marketing surface for a 2-bedroom,
2-bathroom Hollywood Hills Airbnb, plus a guest guidebook and an unlisted brand
guide. Success means visitors understand the property quickly, trust the 4.97
rating and Superhost/Guest Favorite signals, feel the art-filled Hollywood Hills
atmosphere, and click through to reserve on Airbnb.

## Brand Personality

Classic Hollywood glamour, mid-century modernism, and California warmth. The
voice is sophisticated but not corporate, editorial but personal, confident but
not precious. The bungalow should feel like a creative retreat with excellent
taste and a lived-in sense of welcome.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic vacation-rental templates, sterile real-estate pages, overstuffed
amenity grids, glossy luxury cliches, host-personality content, and corporate
hospitality copy. Avoid public exposure of private guest details, exact address
data, or sensitive check-in information.

## Design Principles

Photography is the proof. Lead with the real view, light, materials, art, and
rooms rather than abstract claims.

Make the booking path visible without flattening the cinematic mood. Reserve
actions should stay easy to find, especially on mobile.

Keep privacy part of polish. Guest-only information belongs in Airbnb messages
or private channels, not crawlable pages or structured data.

Use rhythm over templates. Favor full-bleed moments, asymmetric grids, precise
typography, and restrained details over repeated cards.

Every word should earn the stay. Copy should be warm, sensory, short, and free
of host names or first-person host identity.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG AA for contrast and keyboard access wherever possible. Preserve
visible focus states, 44px minimum touch targets, reduced-motion support, and a
usable no-JavaScript fallback. Mobile guidebook use is a core experience, not an
afterthought.
